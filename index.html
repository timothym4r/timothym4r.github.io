About

I am an Indonesian technologist based in Toronto, recently graduated from the University of Toronto. My background spans data science, computer science, machine learning research, applied NLP, analytics, and full-stack engineering.

At UofT, I worked with Aida Ramezani and Prof. Yang Xu on computational moral character inference, studying whether transformer representations of dialogue can predict perceived moral and personality traits. I also worked with Prof. Radu Craiu as a Data Science Researcher on statistical and data science research.

At Bank Negara Indonesia, I worked on applied financial NLP problems such as transaction categorization, sentiment analysis, customer segmentation, and multilingual text processing. I also contributed to an AI Ed-platform at the University of Waterloo associated with Prof. Victor Cui, and built systems like ScriptSense, MindMap, and Scriptorium to explore document AI, knowledge graphs, and full-stack product engineering.

I like the full path from problem to system: define the question, understand the data, choose an approach, test it honestly, and ship something understandable.

  • AI / MLPyTorch, scikit-learn, Hugging Face, BERT-style models, sentence transformers, XGBoost, embeddings, RAG concepts.
  • DataPython, SQL, pandas, NumPy, BigQuery, analytics workflows, visualization, experimentation, and metric design.
  • SoftwareTypeScript, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Django REST Framework, Prisma, Docker, AWS, REST APIs, JWT authentication.

01 / NLP RESEARCH

Moral Character Inference

Investigated whether transformer representations of dialogue can predict how fictional characters are perceived along moral and personality dimensions.

Placeholder image: trait map / embedding plot
BERTRoBERTaDeBERTaAutoencodersEvaluation
Problem
Character perception is subtle, social, and noisy. The research asked which traits are encoded in language strongly enough to be modeled.
Scale
Worked with roughly 300,000 utterances, 201 characters, 97 films, and 500 bipolar trait dimensions.
Approach
Experimented with transformer fine-tuning, sentence embeddings, PCA, ridge regression, and masked-language-model representation learning.
